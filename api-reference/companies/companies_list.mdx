---
title: "Fetch Companies"
description: "Retrieve organizations and companies with roll-up metrics (contacts, active deals, last activity), search, pagination, or fetch an individual company by ID."
api: "GET /companies"
---

### Query Parameters

<ParamField query="id" type="string">
  Optional: Pass a specific company UUID to fetch complete profile details, roll-ups, and associated contacts for that single company directly.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="search" type="string">
  Search keyword matching against company name or website domain.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" type="number">
  Page number for pagination. Defaults to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="number">
  Number of company records to retrieve per page. Defaults to `50`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="sort" type="string">
  Field to sort by: `contacts`, `open_deals`, `last_activity`, `created`, or `name`. Defaults to `contacts`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="dir" type="string">
  Sort direction: `asc` or `desc`. Defaults to `desc`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="owner_id" type="string">
  Filter companies assigned to a specific team member UUID.
</ParamField>
