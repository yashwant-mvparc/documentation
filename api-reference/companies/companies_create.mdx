---
title: "Create Company"
description: "Register a new business account or organization. Automatically deduplicates by corporate domain."
api: "POST /companies"
---

### Request Body

<ParamField body="name" type="string" required>
  The legal or display name of the company (e.g., `Acme Innovations`).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="domain" type="string">
  Corporate email/web domain (e.g., `acme.com`). Automatically cleans and normalizes domains.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="website" type="string">
  Full website URL (e.g., `https://acme.com`). If domain is omitted, domain is inferred from the website URL.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="phone" type="string">
  Primary business phone number.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="industry" type="string">
  Industry or market vertical (e.g., `Fintech`, `Healthcare`, `SaaS`).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="owner_id" type="string">
  UUID of the assigned account executive or team member.
</ParamField>
