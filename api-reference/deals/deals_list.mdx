---
title: "Fetch Deals"
description: "Retrieve deals across pipelines with stage breakdown, filtering, or fetch a specific deal directly by ID."
api: "GET /deals"
---

### Query Parameters

<ParamField query="id" type="string">
  Optional: Pass a specific deal UUID to retrieve full details for that single deal (including its stage, company, primary contact, and contact roster).
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="pipeline_id" type="string">
  Filter deals by a specific pipeline UUID. If omitted, the default sales pipeline is returned.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="stage_id" type="string">
  Filter deals by a specific pipeline stage UUID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="status" type="string">
  Filter by deal status: `open`, `won`, or `lost`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="search" type="string">
  Search keyword across deal title, notes, or associated company name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="owner_id" type="string">
  Filter by assigned team member UUID.
</ParamField>
